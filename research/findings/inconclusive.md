# Inconclusive results

accuracy in leave-one-training-speaker-out cross-validation, compared with 0.817 for the
accepted baseline, but only 0.500 on the two-speaker validation split, tying the baseline.
This is conflicting evidence, not a validated gain. See `research/research_status.md`.
